Beyond Code

Things you can ask it

  • "What does this project do? Give me a summary"
  • "Compare these two competitor landing pages"
  • "Find every place we mention pricing in our docs"
  • "Write user stories from this requirements doc"
  • "Analyze this spreadsheet and flag outliers"
  • "Draft release notes from this list of changes"

Be specific about what you want. Vague asks get vague results.

What I've Learned the Hard Way
It invents facts with total confidence
Fabricated numbers in my slides. Faked article summaries. Called real tools fictional.
You are the quality filter
It won't tell you when it's wrong. If you don't check, you ship confident garbage.
It gets stuck in loops
Same failing approach, five times in a row. You have to notice and interrupt.
You own what it builds
Writes a feature in two minutes. When it breaks at midnight? That's your problem.
It's incredible anyway
I've shipped tools used daily by my team, automated hours of weekly reporting, and built integrations that would have taken months. The tradeoffs are real, but I wouldn't go back.
